Pitch-Injected Residual Adapter for Tonal Language
in Neural Audio Codec

Jie-Shiang Yang, Ya-Tse Wu, Chi-Chun Lee  ·  National Tsing Hua University

Interspeech 2026

📄 Code

Abstract

Neural Audio Codecs (NACs) achieve high-fidelity reconstruction at low bitrates but remain insensitive to fundamental frequency (F0) distortion—a critical limitation for tonal languages, which comprise over 40% of the world’s languages and use F0 contour for lexical distinction. We propose PIRA (Pitch-Injected Residual Adapter), a lightweight plug-and-play module that restores tonal information in frozen NACs through explicit F0 and voiced/unvoiced side-information injection, requiring no codec retraining. With only 1.25M trainable parameters, PIRA reduces codec-induced Tone Error Rate (dTER) by 35.7% on average across three typologically diverse tonal languages and five pretrained codecs, while preserving English quality and adding ≤0.4 kbps bitrate overhead.
